This project documents the design, configuration, and development of a cybersecurity homelab built to support practical digital forensics and incident response work. The environment is intended to provide a controlled and repeatable platform containing segmented networks, monitored endpoints, attacker and victim systems, centralised logging, and supporting security services. This project focuses on the infrastructure itself, including hardware, networking, virtualisation, monitoring, security controls, and validation. Incident investigations, forensic analysis, attack simulations, and case findings will be documented separately as dedicated portfolio work.
